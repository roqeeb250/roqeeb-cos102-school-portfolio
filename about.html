MY STORY

Growing through curiosity and practice.

My journey into technology is driven by a desire to learn, understand how things work, and turn knowledge into something useful. I am currently studying at Miva Open University and developing my skills in web development, one concept and one project at a time.

I would describe myself as gentle, easygoing, and naturally shy. I may not always be the loudest person in the room, but I enjoy learning, exploring new ideas, and finding ways to improve myself. I believe growth can happen quietly through patience, practice, and the willingness to keep trying.

Outside my studies, I enjoy reading, watching educational courses, and building meaningful connections with people. These interests encourage me to expand my knowledge and learn from different perspectives.

As I grow in technology, I want to move beyond simply learning how things work. I want to apply what I learn by building websites, solving problems, and creating projects that have lasting value.

I know that becoming a good developer takes time, patience, and consistent effort. I see myself as a work in progress, with skills to develop, ideas to explore, and goals to pursue. My aim is simple: keep learning, keep building, and turn curiosity into practical solutions.
